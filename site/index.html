SteamRoller

Custom firmware for the Novation Circuit Rhythm

 

  1. Switch the Circuit Rhythm off.
  2. Hold Sample Rec, Samples and Note while you switch it on.
  3. Connect it to this computer by USB.

Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Help
  • Close other music apps (and Novation Components) before connecting.
  • When the browser asks to control MIDI devices, allow it.
  • Keep the USB cable and power connected until the page says the install is done.
  • To go back to the original firmware, use Novation Components.

if something goes wrong.

Unofficial firmware, not made or supported by Novation. Use it at your own risk and back up your projects first.
