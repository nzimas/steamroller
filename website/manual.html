Manual

Everything SteamRoller adds to the Circuit Rhythm. All of the Rhythm's own functions work as described in Novation's user guide; this manual only covers what is new or different. To install, see Install.

The sound generator

In the Sample Rec view, three pads of the third row control the generator. Each sound is synthesized on the Rhythm by an FM drum voice, so every press gives a new one.

Pad (third row)FunctionLight
third from the rightKit: tap to make a page of 16 sounds; hold 1.5 s to clear the pagedim purple; blinks while it works

rightmostCommit mode, on and offdim red; bright while on, yellow while storing, orange when a store can't start

Only one mode is on at a time. With neither on, the view behaves as stock.

Generate mode

Press a track button for a new sound of that family, played on that track's output. Press again for another. Shift + track button plays the current sound again. The track buttons don't leave the Sample Rec view while this mode is on.

Storing a sound

Turn commit mode on. A track button now plays that track's sound again. To store it, hold the track button and press a slot pad (rows 1–2, the page shown). The sound is written into that slot of the current pack, replacing what was there in every project that uses the pack, and plays once from the slot. Record does not need to be on.

Stored sounds last until they fade out (at most 2 seconds), are normalized to −1 dBFS, and are exactly what you auditioned. If the store can't start (no sound on that track, a store already running, or recording), the commit pad flashes orange and nothing is written.

Kits

Tap the kit pad to fill the page shown with 16 new sounds, replacing that page's slots.

  • In a pack with no generated sounds, the page is a balanced starter kit: kicks, snares, a clap, an impact, hats, a cymbal, metal, noise, a click and a destroyed sound, grouped by rows.
  • To extend the kit, go to another page and tap again. The new page is planned from every generated sound already in the pack. Each page has its own focus: the three or four kinds of sound the pack has least of fill most of it and lead its first row, with a few others around them. Within each kind, the rarer characters come first. Over all eight pages the kit evens out, up to 128 sounds.
  • Each generated sample carries a small tag in its file, so this works across power-offs. Samples you recorded yourself are not counted.
  • Hold the kit pad 1.5 s to clear the page shown (its 16 slots).

Parameter locks

With Record on, hold a step and turn a knob: the value is stored on that step. Turning a knob while nothing is held changes the track's own value, as stock. A lock is sent a moment before its step and written straight to the sound chip, so it is in place when the sound starts. Locks save with the project.

To remove a knob's locks, hold Clear and turn that knob: this clears its whole lane on the track.

How long a lock lasts

  • A lock lasts for the whole sound of its step: the steps that trigger nothing keep it, so a kick panned right stays right for its whole tail.
  • The next hit without a lock returns the parameter to the knob's value.
  • Locks belong to the step's content: deleting a step deletes its locks, and copying a step copies them.

Step sliders

With Record on, hold a step in a track's normal view: the two bottom pad rows stop showing samples and become sliders for that step. Two rows show at a time. While still holding the step, the sample-page arrows scroll through the rows. When you let go of the step, the rows go back to the top (level and pan).

A bright pad or bar is the step's lock; a dim one shows the track's own value. Press a pad to set the lock; press the pad that shows the lock again to remove it. With Record off, holding a step and pressing a sample pad is the stock sample flip.

Grid FX

Below reverb come seven rows, one per Grid FX, in the colours of the Rhythm's own Grid FX view. Each row's eight pads are eight settings, more intense (or faster) to the right. Rates follow the project's tempo. A step can lock several effects at once.

RowColourPads, left to right

Only the track that holds the lock is affected. While a locked hit plays, its track alone goes through the Grid FX and every other track bypasses them. The effect lasts until that track's next hit without Grid FX locks, or until you stop. When two tracks hold Grid FX locks at the same time, the newest lock takes over and the earlier track goes back to dry.

With no Grid FX locks playing, every track goes through the Grid FX as usual, so the Grid FX view works on the whole mix as always.

Spice

Shift + a track button adds random locks to that track's current pattern. (In the Sample Rec view with generate mode on, Shift + track button replays a sound instead.)

Before adding anything, it reads the pattern: its length, which hits repeat in every bar (the backbone), which change (fills), accents and ghost notes, rolls, how much room each hit has, and what the other tracks play on the same step. It then picks three or four parameters (always pan or level, plus a distortion or filter control) and moves them by role:

  • backbone downbeats keep their place and level;
  • ghost notes get quieter, darker, thinner or panned;
  • fills build, with a crescendo or the filter opening;
  • delay and reverb throws go on hits with room after them, such as the pattern's last hit.

Hits that repeat get the same move every bar, so the groove keeps its shape. Pitch, decay, attack and sample start and end are never touched, and your own locks are kept. Press again for another take: the pattern first goes back to how it was before. Press Play once after loading a project, before the first spice.

Differences from the official firmware

  • Level and pan move faster. The Rhythm smooths every level and pan change over about 100 ms; SteamRoller shortens that to about 5 ms (still smooth) so that locks land on the transient. Mixer moves follow your hand more tightly.
  • Grid FX and the returns. The Grid FX now process the tracks only, not the reverb and delay returns or the audio input.
  • Mixer lanes. Level, pan, delay and reverb automation plays as locks, like every other lane.
  • Recording automation writes one value per step (no glides between knob moves).

Going back

To return to the official firmware, start the Rhythm in update mode (hold Sample Rec, Samples and Note while switching on) and reinstall the firmware with Novation Components. Your projects, packs and stored sounds stay on the device; locks added with SteamRoller play as plain automation there.
