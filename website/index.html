A firmware for the Novation Circuit Rhythm for rhythmic noise, IDM and experimental percussion

Install via browser Read the manual

SteamRoller keeps everything the Circuit Rhythm does: the sampler, the sequencer, the mixer, the effects, your packs and projects. It adds a sound generator that fills your packs with new drum sounds, parameter locks that work on every step the way Elektron's do, a single gesture that varies a whole pattern, and the Grid FX locked to single steps of single tracks.

Features

Sound generator
In the Sample Rec view, the track buttons draw new drum sounds from eight families: kick, snare, hi-hat and cymbal, metal, noise, glitch, impact and gnarly. Each sound is synthesized on the Rhythm (an FM drum voice) and auditioned at once.
Store as samples
Hold a track button and press a slot: the sound becomes a real sample in the current pack, exactly as you heard it.
Kits
One pad fills a whole page of 16 slots with a balanced kit. Each further page extends the kit with what it lacks most, up to 128 sounds.
Parameter locks
Hold a step, turn a knob: the value plays on that step and lasts for its whole sound. The next unlocked hit returns to the knob. Locks land on the transient, not a moment after it.
Step sliders
With Record on, holding a step turns the two bottom pad rows into sliders for that step's level, pan, delay and reverb, and for the seven Grid FX.
Grid FX per step, per track
Bit crusher, stutter, looper, auto-filter, vinyl, reverser and phaser, each with eight settings from gentle to extreme, locked to single steps. Only the track that holds the lock goes through the effect.
Spice
Shift + a track button adds random locks to the track's pattern after reading its structure: the backbone stays, ghost notes move, fills build, throws go where there is room. Press again for another take.
Locks follow the step
Delete a step and its locks go with it; copy a step and they come along. Everything saves with the project.
Installed from the browser
Chrome or Edge, a USB cable, two clicks. Going back to the official firmware takes the same steps in Novation Components.
